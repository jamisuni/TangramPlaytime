**Touch routing** (TASK-013; the badge branch is TASK-014's). Pinned untokened, decisions.md line 59.
1. While a drag runs, every further touch is a twist finger for that drag (S-7 → `twistDrag`), whatever it lands on. Lifting it keeps the turn reached.
2. Otherwise a new touch belongs to the first hit of: a badge's 60 dp area → a tray cell (the whole cell, REQ-015) → a placed piece's shape grown by 12 dp (REQ-015; inside a shape beats another piece's margin; the last-locked piece is on top) → nothing. *Superseded 2026-10-12 (code review F1, `decisions.md` rows of that date): the visible thing wins on both surfaces — a badge's 38 dp disc → a piece's drawn shape (the PG miniature in its cell; a placed piece on the board) → the rest of the badge's 60 dp area (wholly inside the PG cell in the tray) → the rest of a tray cell / a placed piece's 12 dp margin → nothing.*
3. A touch that ends having moved < 12 dp (S-8) is a tap: `pressBadge`, `tapTray` or `tapBoard`. Otherwise, from a cell or a piece, it is a drag: `startDrag` at the drawn centre, then `moveDrag` each frame, with the piece's lowest point 30 dp above the finger (REQ-014) and `overBoard` = the finger is over the board. A touch that starts on a badge never drags.
4. A release calls `drop()`; a pointer cancel calls `goHome()`.

  - **Touch area:** the pill itself. REQ-037's rule exempts the #DevTools controls.

  - For each `SolvedPiece` of the open puzzle, its polygon in px through `BoardMapping` (G-17).
  - The fill is `PlayPalette.piece(id)` at 55 % alpha. The edge is white, 2 dp, dashed 6 dp on and 4 dp off, with round joins.
  - The label is `id.name`, white, 14 sp bold, centred on the polygon's average.
  - It is not touchable.
  - It is not drawn while the open puzzle is Solved. `solutionShown` is kept, so the overlay returns on the next unsolved puzzle.
- **The sheet.** As the overview is built:
  - The scrim (`PlayPalette.scrim`) and the sheet start below the top bar. The bar stays drawn and inert.
  - The sheet is centred, inset 16 dp, at most 360 dp wide, in the paper colour, with 26 dp corners. Its pills are at least 48 dp high.
  - **Prompt:** the title, the hint, the field, the message line, Cancel and OK. The field shows masked digits, uses the system's number keyboard, and takes focus when the prompt opens. A wrong passcode empties it.
  - **Menu:** the title, the unlocked hint, Show (or Hide) the solution, Solve this puzzle now, the no-best-time hint, and Done.
  - A tap on the scrim, Cancel or Done closes it (`close()`). Show, Hide and Solve close it before they act (S-26). Back is not handled (planner question 6).
- **Z-order,** bottom to top: board → silhouette → **the overlay** → Restart pill → board pieces → solved picture → board badge → tray strip, cells, ghosts, miniatures and size marks → tray badge → solved row → **the DEV button** → landing preview → dragged piece → corner pulse → confetti → overview → **the sheet**.
- **Hit order** (S-22's, with the aid):
  0. The overview **or the aid's sheet** is open: the router neither consumes nor acts, on the top bar too. The sheet's `clickable`s and its field act.
  1. A drag is running: every further touch is a twist finger, a finger on the DEV button included.
  2. A top-bar column, or a third of the solved row.
  3. **The DEV button.** It is drawn above every resting piece and badge, and the visible thing wins. Then the badge disc → a piece's drawn shape → the Restart pill → the rest of the badge area → a cell or a margin.
- **Taps.** The DEV button is a control: it never drags. It acts at the lift within the 12 dp slop, whatever the duration. A slide of the slop or more cancels it, and it has no long press (S-22 "Taps and the long press").

- **An activity recreation** keeps the aid object. `DevAidHook.installed` is built once per process, so `unlocked`, `solutionShown`, the open sheet and its message survive; what was typed in the field may be lost. The controller keeps the rest (WO-004). A celebration that an aid solve started is a controller cue and is not replayed (S-3Δ).
- **A process death** builds a new aid: locked, the overlay off, no sheet. This is "until the app is restarted", REQ-046 rule 2 as read (the pending draft's wording). An aid-solved puzzle is in the store as Solved with its best time unchanged. It therefore opens Solved: the picture, the solved row, and the best time "–".
- **Leaving the app** without a process death changes nothing in the aid.
