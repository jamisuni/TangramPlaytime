# CHG — "Available" names the place where the times are shown

## Change
REQ-005 Acceptance A1: **"After solving a puzzle, its solve time is available."** becomes **"After solving a puzzle, its solve time is shown on the solved view (REQ-026) and in the settings list (REQ-030)."**
REQ-005 Acceptance A2: **"The total active play time is available at any time."** becomes **"Today's and the all-time active play time are shown in the settings screen (REQ-029) whenever it is opened."**

## Reason
Review-01 F9 (Should). "Available" is a lock-gate-v2 vague term; the lines name no observable.

## Impact
REQ-005.A1, REQ-005.A2. Build side: none beyond pointing the acceptance test at REQ-026/REQ-029 screens.
