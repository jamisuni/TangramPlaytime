# Asset licences — TangramPlaytime

Every sound, font and image the game ships is listed here with its licence (`architecture.md` G-16, v1.2). The game itself is GPL-3.0 (`LICENSE`). Assets are made for this project or licence-free. **Puzzle figures**, the arrangement of the seven pieces in a puzzle's `solution`, may also come from a source under the app's own licence, GPL-3.0. Each such figure is listed below by puzzle id with its collection, source figure id and licence, and its collection's credit is in § Sources. Its picture (`art`) is still made for this project. Later work orders add their rows here (the sounds: WO-007).

The table of puzzle figures lists **every** `Tangrams/*.json` file, the project's own included, so that a missing row can be seen (`decisions.md` 2026-10-19 "`ASSET-LICENCES.md` lists every puzzle, kept complete by a JVM pin").

## Pictures, fonts, sounds

- **Pictures:** every puzzle picture (`art`) is made for this project. A picture not drawn yet carries the one-colour stub `{"base": "#D9DCE8", "shapes": []}` (WO-010, until its batch draws it).
- **Fonts:** the system font only; no font is bundled (ADR-005).
- **Sounds:** none yet (WO-007 adds the sounds and their rows).
