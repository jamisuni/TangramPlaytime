# Tangram Playtime — privacy policy

Privacy: this game collects no data. It has no network access, no accounts and no analytics. Your puzzle progress and play times are stored only on this device and are deleted when the game is uninstalled.

This is the text the game itself shows in its settings screen.

## What this means

- **No data is collected or shared.** The game sends nothing off your device, to the developer or to anyone else.
- **No account.** There is nothing to sign up for and nothing to sign in to.
- **No network access.** The game does not ask for internet access, nor for any other permission.
- **No analytics and no ads.** The game contains no analytics and no advertising.
- **Only on your device.** Your puzzle progress, your settings and your play times are kept on your device, in the game's private storage. Uninstalling the game deletes them.
- **Children.** Nothing is collected from anyone, children included.

## Contact

Write to the developer e-mail address shown on the game's Google Play page, or open an issue at https://github.com/jamisuni/TangramPlaytime/issues.
