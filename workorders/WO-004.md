# WO-004 — #Browsing + `:store`: moving between puzzles, and progress that survives a restart

**Status:** Done (closed 2026-10-17, G4 under governance row 9)  ·  **Mode:** Full (c)
**Kicked off by:** Jami / 2026-10-15 (KICKOFF.md paste-in brief)  ·  **Plan approved:** 2026-10-15 19:52 (G3 under governance row 7 `ai`; plan-check GREEN; design review 0 B / 5 S; plan review 0 B / 3 S)

## Goal

Make the Play screen a library the player moves through, and make what they
did there survive closing the app. On the Play screen of `:play`: the top
bar's ‹ › and counter, Restart and Retry, the solved view, the long press on
› and the grid overview (#Browsing). Behind it, the `:store` module
implementing `IProgressStore`, so `PlayController`'s in-memory per-puzzle state
(WO-003 seams S-1…S-3) is persisted and the app reopens where it was left.
Fourth in the owner's order (`build-map.md` §2). Two items carried from
WO-003 land here because ‹ › and the grid are the first callers of `open()`
and the store read sits at the bundle's load point (`decisions.md`
2026-10-12, "Carried to WO-004 #Browsing").

| 1 | **Restart takes one press, with no "are you sure?".** *What it is:* the Restart pill (shown only while a puzzle is half-done) puts every piece back in the tray. *What happened:* the requirement says pressing Restart restarts; it names no confirmation. A child can tap it by accident and lose the board. *Proposal:* one press restarts, no question; the pill sits at the board's top-left and a piece drawn over it wins the touch. *What you give up:* an accidental tap costs the board (the puzzle itself is never locked or lost). *Recommended:* one press. | one press, no confirmation | 2026-10-15 | answered 2026-10-17 — default confirmed | decisions.md 2026-10-15 "The top bar, Restart and the solved row, as drawn" |
| 2 | **The Retry · best time · Next row comes after the picture is fully shown.** *What it is:* when the last piece locks, the tray is replaced by Retry, the best time and Next. *What happened:* no requirement says when. *Proposal:* the row appears once the picture has faded in, about 1.4 s after the last piece, so the celebration always plays and a frantic tap cannot skip it; a puzzle opened already solved shows the row at once. *What you give up:* about 1.4 s before Next can be pressed after a solve. *Recommended:* after the picture. | after the picture (≈ 1.4 s) | 2026-10-15 | answered 2026-10-17 — default confirmed | same row |
