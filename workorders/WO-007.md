## Goal

Build the one settings screen the owner asked for ("maybe settings menu on
top", SRC-002): a gear in the top bar opens it over the current puzzle, and
it holds, in REQ-032's order, the timer switch, the sound switch, today's and
all-time play time with the best times, Reset all progress behind a second
confirmation, a short how-to-play text, the free note and the privacy text.
The puzzle time pauses while it is open; closing it leaves the board as it
was. The game plays short sounds for its five actions and a haptic tick on a
lock, both silenced by the sound switch. WO-008 (closed 2026-10-21) built the
counting this screen shows; the last WO after this one is WO-009 #Release.

## Constraints & notes

**Questions the design must settle** (each reading no REQ fixes becomes a `decisions.md` row through the orchestrator; product-visible ones may become checkpoint items, at most five):

1. **The screen and its place.** Over the current puzzle (REQ-032), inside the activity's window (carry 2), REQ-032 rule 1's order, a phone and a tablet in either orientation the layout allows (TYPE-007; REQ-035, REQ-036), scrolling where it does not fit, how it closes (a control on the screen, and Back — rule 3), the gear's place in the top bar and its touch area (REQ-037, `design-inputs.md` §2 item 6). What REQ-032.A1 "changes nothing on the board" covers: placements, the puzzle's state, the open puzzle, its time (the pause).
2. **The pause** (REQ-032 rule 2, REQ-030 rule 1): the controller's predicate term, the folds on open and close (carry 2); play time keeps counting while the screen is open, and touches on it count (`decisions.md` 2026-10-20 "What counts on each screen"). 3. **The two switches** (REQ-031 values off / on, default off; REQ-033, sound on by default — its ASSUMPTION and `Settings.soundOn = true`). Written through `:play`'s one writer (`Persistence`, ADR-003) to `IProgressStore.saveSettings`; carry 1 (N1) — the board's timer changes at once, without a restart.
4. **The times on the screen** (REQ-029.A2; REQ-030 rule 3 "in the settings list"; REQ-032 rule 1 "play time and best times"): today's and all-time from `IActiveTime.playTimes()`, whether they move while the screen is open, D10's one format (`timeOf` public, carry 2). 6. **Sounds and the haptic tick** (REQ-033): the five events from O-07's stream (`PickedUp`, `Turned`, `Locked`, `WentHome`, `Solved`), each at most 1 s, no sound that signals failure (the return is a soft low tone), the tick on a lock only, through the permission-free API (G-11), both following the switch.
