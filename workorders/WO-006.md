## Goal

Make the game fit every Android screen the Contract names and prove what it promises. In `:app` (O-05): the TYPE-007 window class picks the Phone layout (portrait only, the tray in two rows) or the Tablet layout (both orientations, one wide tray row); the screen is drawn edge to edge, the tray clears the gesture bar and every control respects the insets; Back closes an open overlay, otherwise leaves the app. Every player control gets a touch area of at least 48 dp (‹ › 52 dp, the flip badge 60 dp). Every text has an English resource (the Contract wording) and a Finnish one (a reference translation), and the language follows the device. Finally the promise lines — no ad, no price, no money ask, no rating prompt, no permission, no network — get honest oracles on the release APK and on a play-through. Sixth in the owner's order (`build-map.md` §2).
