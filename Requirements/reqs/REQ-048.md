# REQ-048 — Store obligations of a free children's game

## Statement
The released game SHALL meet the obligations of the store it is published in for an audience that includes children, without adding any network use, data collection or permission.

## Rationale
Google Play requires a privacy policy of every app, even one that collects nothing, in the Play Console and inside the app (SRC-012); a children's audience brings the Families policies (SRC-007); an unrated app is not permitted (SRC-013). The owner accepted the release checklist (SRC-011, G2) and chose the store name (SRC-011, D9).

## Rules
- Store name: **"Tangram Playtime"** (SRC-016; Play's metadata policy bars "free" in a title — CHG-001). The listing's short description carries the promise "absolutely free — no ads, nothing to buy", and the promise stays inside the game (REQ-001, REQ-009). The repository keeps its name.
- The app has a launcher icon; the store icon is made from it. The icon is chosen by the owner from options proposed to him (SRC-016, 2026-10-05: option A, a coloured square, kept as design input DI-9); it shows no text and no third-party mark (CHG-002).
- A privacy policy exists as a hosted text for the store's field and as a text inside the game (REQ-049); both say that the game collects nothing.
- The store's data-safety declaration says "no data collected", which REQ-010 makes true.
- The content rating questionnaire is completed before release.
- The target audience declared includes children under 13, so the Families policies apply: no location permission, no unapproved third-party code, no ads.
- The game is offered to the store's teacher-approved programme where one exists.
- Nothing in this REQ adds a permission, a network call or a third-party component.

## Acceptance
- **A1** — The store listing shows a privacy policy link, no "Contains ads" and no "In-app purchases" label (REQ-001).
- **A2** — The listing's title is "Tangram Playtime" and its short description states the free, no-ads promise.
- **A3** — The installed app still declares no permissions (REQ-010).
- **A4** — The installed app shows a launcher icon that matches the store icon; the icon contains no text.

## Out of scope
Which store or channel (Play, F-Droid, a signed file): an open question in the index. Technology.

## Assumptions
- ASSUMPTION: the store title is "Tangram Playtime" in both store languages (the owner accepted CHG-001 on 2026-10-11 without naming a Finnish form); the Finnish short description carries the promise in Finnish.
- ASSUMPTION: the hosted privacy policy is a page in the project's repository.
