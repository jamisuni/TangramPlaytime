Tangram Playtime is a tangram puzzle game. Rebuild a shape from the classic seven tangram pieces: five triangles, a square and a parallelogram.

Drag a piece from the tray onto the shape. Tap a piece to turn it, or twist it with a second finger while you drag. The badge beside the parallelogram flips it. A piece you drop snaps into place or goes back to the tray. Fill the whole shape to see its picture.

The challenge is in the puzzle, not in the controls. The game is made for players aged 8 and up.

Enjoy, it's absolutely free. No ads, no purchases, no network. Your play time stays on this device. Nothing is for sale, nothing is locked, and nobody is asked for money.

Nothing leaves your device. The game works without an internet connection and asks for no permissions. It collects no data: no accounts, no analytics. Your puzzle progress and play times are stored only on your device and are deleted when the game is uninstalled.

In the settings you can switch the on-screen timer and the sound on or off, and see your play time and your best times.

Play on a phone held upright or on a tablet, with a layout made for each.

The game follows your device's language: Finnish when the device is set to Finnish, English otherwise.
